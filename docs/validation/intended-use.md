# Intended Use Statement — Regula

> **SPEC-REGULA-VALIDATION-001 (REQ-VAL-001, AC-1)** · Version-controlled declaration
> of intended use, prohibited use, and the human review boundary for the Regula
> regulatory-affairs decision-support system. This document is the authoritative
> scope-of-use reference cited by every Release Validation Report
> (`docs/validation/release-report-<release_id>.md`).
>
> Regulatory anchors: **GAMP 5 / CSV** (IQ/OQ/PQ qualification framework),
> **21 CFR Part 11 §11.10(i)** (computer-system validation documentation),
> **ISO 13485:2016 §4.1.6** (software validation + change control),
> **ISO 14971:2019** (residual-risk record). CSV-lite internal governance scope —
> not an FDA-submission software validation package (see §3 Exclusions).

## §1 Intended Use (Permitted)

Regula is a **decision-support tool** for qualified regulatory-affairs
professionals. The following uses are within the validated scope:

1. **RA decision support** — chat-based retrieval of regulatory sources with
   citation and confidence scoring, used to inform (not replace) human RA
   judgement.
2. **Submission draft assistance** — workflow drafts for 510(k), CER (Clinical
   Evaluation Report), and PCCP (Predetermined Change Control Plan). Drafts are
   intermediate work product; they require human review and approval before any
   external use.
3. **Risk-management documentation assistance** — drafting support for risk
   registers and FMEA worksheets. Outputs are inputs to the human-owned quality
   process, not final artifacts.
4. **Expert-review workbench** — structured queue for RA-lead / QA-lead
   review of AI-generated answers, including citation post-processing and
   confidence-based auto-flagging.
5. **Submission-package evidence bundling** — assembly of linked evidence
   (citations, drafts, review records) into a reviewable package. Export is a
   convenience for human reviewers; Regula does **not** submit directly to any
   regulator.

## §2 Prohibited Use

The following uses are **out of scope** and must be blocked by policy, training,
and (where feasible) automated gating:

1. **Autonomous regulatory decisions** — any final regulatory judgement made
   without a human reviewer's sign-off. Every RA claim requires a citation and a
   reviewer before it may leave the system.
2. **Medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations** — Regula is not a medical
   device. It does not interpret patient data and does not provide clinical
   advice.
3. **PHI / PII processing** — Regula must not receive protected health
   information or personally identifying information. The PHI-removal pipeline
   (SPEC-REGULA-PHI-REMOVAL-001) is a precondition for any document ingestion.
4. **Direct submission to regulators** — Regula may not file, transmit, or
   otherwise directly hand deliverables to the FDA, EMA, MFDS, NMPA, PMDA, or
   any other authority. Human review and an explicit approver sign-off are
   mandatory before any external distribution.
5. **Bypass of the export gate** — auto-generated drafts may not leave the
   system until the human-review gate has recorded a reviewer's approval.

## §3 Human Review Boundary

- **Every RA claim is contingent.** No claim generated by Regula may be used in
  a submission, risk record, or external communication without (a) a verifiable
  citation and (b) a documented human reviewer sign-off.
- **Auto-flagging is binding.** Low-confidence answers (`confidence < 0.7`) and
  policy-keyword triggers (e.g. clinical-trial exemption, emergency use) are
  routed to the expert-review queue automatically. Reviewers may resolve, but
  not silently dismiss, a flag.
- **Reviewer accountability** is defined in SPEC-REGULA-REVIEW-OPS-001 (#36).
  The reviewer's id, timestamp, and decision are recorded in the audit log and
  linked from every released artifact.
- **Sign-off is the final gate.** A Release Validation Report may be signed off
  only when every checklist item is met (REQ-VAL-013) and every high-impact
  change has either rerun evidence or a justified residual-risk record
  (REQ-VAL-008).

## §4 Exclusions (Out-of-Scope Validations)

This document is **not** any of the following:

- An FDA 510(k) software validation deliverable.
- A SOC 2 / ISO 27001 attestation.
- A full QMS (Quality Management System) workflow.
- An audit-log hash-chain specification (that is owned by
  SPEC-V3-AUDIT-CHAIN-001 / PR #356; this document consumes the chain).
- An LLM/prompt change-management procedure (owned by
  SPEC-REGULA-MODEL-GOVERNANCE-001 / #71; the release impact assessment
  consumes its outputs as one of seven axes).
